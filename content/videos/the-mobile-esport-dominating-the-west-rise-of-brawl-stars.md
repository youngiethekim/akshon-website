Competitive mobile games have traditionally had a difficult time finding a foothold in western countries, let alone becoming an esport, but there is one title that has bucked the trend and has become a breakout success, Brawl Stars.

Following the game's global launch in 2018, Supercell's community-oriented approach to creating a competitive ecosystem has allowed Brawl Stars to come into its own as an esports title with nearly 400,000 peak viewers tuning in to watch the Brawl Stars World Finals 2022.
