# OWL Pacific Division Press Conference at All-Star Weekend

The Overwatch League All-Star Weekend brought together the best talent from the Pacific Division for an exclusive press conference. This lighthearted sit-down featured players and coaches opening up about their All-Star experience, memorable moments, and their plans for the OWL off-season. Fans got rare access to their favorite players in a relaxed setting where genuine conversations happened outside the intensity of competitive play.

## OWL Pacific Division All-Star Roster Highlights

The Pacific Division press conference showcased some of the league's most iconic names. Dynasty's Fissure, Ryujehong, and Fleta represented their team's legacy. Valiant brought Custa, Fate, Space, and SoOn, while Shock's Architect and Sleepy joined the conversation. Gladiators' Surefour and BigGoose, Fuel's OGE and Mickie, and Dragons' Geguri rounded out an all-star lineup. Each player reflected on competing at the highest level and the significance of being selected for All-Star recognition.

The event celebrated the region's competitive excellence. Players discussed the unique experience of stepping outside their normal team dynamics to compete alongside all-stars from across the Pacific. Legendary figures like Seagull, who has since retired from professional play, added historical context to the celebration.

## Off-Season Plans and Career Reflections

Players shared their off-season priorities, from rest and recovery to streaming and content creation. Many discussed their goals for the next season, potential roster movements, and what fans can expect in coming months. The off-season is crucial for players to recharge mentally and physically while staying engaged with the community through various content platforms.

## Coach Moon's Leadership Perspective

Valiant's head coach Moon provided insights into player development and the strategic thinking behind Pacific Division success. His perspective on talent scouting and in-game leadership gave viewers a behind-the-scenes look at what makes top teams click. Coach Moon emphasized the importance of player morale and team cohesion during the competitive season.

The press conference wrapped up with lighthearted banter and insider stories that only fellow competitors would appreciate. This candid format revealed the personalities behind the pro players and their genuine camaraderie across rival teams.
