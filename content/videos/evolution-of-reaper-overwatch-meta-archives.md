# Reaper Evolution in Overwatch Meta

Reaper, aka Gabriel Reyes, has been a cornerstone of Overwatch since launch. This shadow-shrouded shotgun wielder's **evolution** reveals how Blizzard fine-tuned a close-range menace into a genuinely terrifying presence. Understanding **Reaper's evolution** and balance changes shows how one character defined multiple metas over years of iteration.

From his earliest concept art in Project Titan to his current form, Reaper's core identity remained unchanged: close-range dominance and tank melting. His signature dual Hellfire Shotguns deal up to 120 damage per shot, eliminating most heroes in two hits when positioned correctly. But raw damage alone doesn't explain why Reaper became a meta staple—it's the adjustments to his abilities that transformed him from one-dimensional threat to genuine flanking menace.

## Early Reaper: Soul Orbs & Clunky Mechanics

At launch, Reaper earned health by picking up Soul Orbs after eliminations. His Wraith Form couldn't reload and forced him into vulnerability. Shadow Step required standing still—a sitting duck scenario—while announcing his arrival with a map-wide voice line. These mechanics made early Reaper a pure damage dealer who sacrificed mobility for output.

## Game-Changing Buffs: 2017–2019

June 2017 introduced The Reaping, a lifesteal passive replacing Soul Orbs. Reaper now healed 20% of all hero damage dealt, enabling sustained dueling without kill requirements. May 2017 made Wraith Form reload instantly, allowing aggressive repositioning. By April 2019, Shadow Step became airborne-castable, removing the animation lock that made Reaper vulnerable.

These changes transformed him from a one-dimensional threat into a slippery flanker who could escape bad fights and reposition for devastating ultimate plays.

## The Beyblade Meta: Reaper's Moment of Glory

Season 2 saw Reaper reach peak oppressiveness alongside Ana's speed-boosting Nano Boost. Players discovered that nanoboosted Reaper, spinning Death Blossom with Zarya bubbles, was nearly unstoppable. The "Beyblade" composition (Reinhardt, Zarya, Reaper, Mei, Ana, Lucio) dominated matches until Blizzard removed Nano Boost's speed multiplier, cementing Reaper as a legitimate threat when conditions favored him.

## Modern Adjustments: The Lifesteal Rollercoaster

Reaper's lifesteal has been Blizzard's biggest balancing challenge. January 2019 increased it to 50%—completely broken. Roadhog hooks meant Roadhog's death, not Reaper's. After two months of chaos, they dialed it to manageable levels around 30–35%. February 2021 changed his spread pattern, reducing damage-per-pellet but extending effective range, making him viable beyond point-blank range.

## Why Reaper Remains Essential

Despite nerfs and constant iteration, Reaper's simplicity masks incredible depth. New players understand his shotguns instantly, but mastering Wraith Form timing, Shadow Step positioning, and Death Blossom setup separates good from exceptional Reaper players. His weakness to burst damage and range disadvantage keep him balanced.
