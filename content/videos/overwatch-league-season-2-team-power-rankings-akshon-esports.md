# Overwatch League Season 2 Power Rankings

Overwatch League Season 2 is bringing major changes to the competitive scene. With eight new franchises joining the original 12 teams, the landscape has shifted dramatically. Some organizations maintained their core rosters, while others executed complete rebuilds. Our Overwatch League Season 2 power rankings analyze how these roster moves position each team heading into the new season.

## Understanding the OWL Season 2 Roster Changes

The inaugural season established strong foundations for many teams, but Season 2 demanded aggressive roster decisions. Teams evaluated their starting lineups, made strategic pickups, and in some cases, overhauled their entire competitive roster. These moves reflect each organization's commitment to building championship-caliber squads that can compete at the highest level.

## Top-Tier Teams and Championship Contenders

The elite teams in our Overwatch League Season 2 power rankings feature organizations that either maintained proven rosters or made high-impact acquisitions. These franchises have the depth, coaching infrastructure, and player synergy necessary to make deep playoff runs. They've avoided major roster instability and retain the mechanical skill and game understanding that defined Season 1 success.

## Mid-Tier Contenders Looking to Prove Themselves

A significant cluster of teams sit in the middle tier, capable of stealing wins but not yet proven as consistent championship threats. These squads feature promising talent mixed with veteran presence, creating unpredictable matchups. Some benefited from smart free-agency moves, while others are banking on coaching adjustments and team chemistry development.

## Rising Teams and New Franchises

The eight new franchises represent both opportunity and risk in Overwatch League Season 2. While some have assembled impressive rosters through the expansion draft and strategic signings, others face the challenge of building competitive teams from scratch. These teams could produce surprise contenders or struggle with integration issues—making them the most volatile element in our power rankings.

## Lower-Tier Teams with Rebuilding Paths

Some organizations in our Overwatch League Season 2 power rankings are clearly in transition, having made significant roster changes that suggest long-term rebuilding efforts. These teams may struggle in the short term but could emerge as threats if their young talent develops and coaching strategies take hold.
