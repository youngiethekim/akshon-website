# Gigantic Is Back From The Dead And Better Than Ever

After six years in the graveyard, **Gigantic Rampage Edition** is officially returning. Gearbox's hero shooter revival launches April 9th, 2024, and it's addressing everything fans loved—plus plenty they've been asking for.

## What's New In Gigantic Rampage Edition

The comeback isn't just nostalgia. Rampage Edition includes all legacy content from the original game, two fresh heroes (Roland and Kajer), and two new maps (Picaro Bay and Heaven's Ward). The standout addition is **Rush mode**, a faster-paced alternative designed to lower the skill floor and hook new players quickly. A ranked mode launches alongside casual play, giving competitive enthusiasts the ranked ladder they've craved.

## Quality-of-Life Improvements That Matter

Beyond content drops, Gearbox partnered with **Abstraction Games**—the studio behind Baldur's Gate 3 and Mass Effect Legendary Edition—to overhaul the new player experience. The revamped tutorial actually teaches mechanics instead of throwing players into the deep end. Crossplay support means you're not locked to your platform, and the dev team has addressed countless polish issues that plagued the original.

## The Business Model Changed

Here's what turned the community's head: Gigantic Rampage Edition ditches the free-to-play monetization trap. The game costs $20 upfront, and **there are no microtransactions**. All cosmetics and content unlock through gameplay. That decision alone signals the developers aren't chasing whales—they're building a game people actually want to play long-term.

## The Competition Is Real

Yes, Overwatch and Paladins still exist. Yes, both games are past their peak. But Helldivers 2's recent success proves players will flock to well-designed multiplayer games that respect their time and money. Gigantic's early community response has been overwhelmingly positive, and the development roadmap shows genuine commitment to preventing the mistakes that killed the original.

The real question isn't whether Gigantic can survive—it's whether Abstraction Games maintains momentum post-launch. Watch the full video to see hands-on gameplay and hear why this revival might be the comeback story the gaming community needs right now.
