Following the popularity of Apex Legends, Fortnite, and PUBG, it did not take long for the battle royale genre to become saturated as developers looked to emulate what looked like a winning formula.

The basic concept of making a game with dozens of players placed in a gradually shrinking battlefield with the win condition of taking out the competition to be the last man standing was replicated across many games, but there is one title that took movement and combat in a different direction opting for a focus on melee action instead of gunplay.

This is the story of Naraka Bladepoint, the best melee battle royale.
