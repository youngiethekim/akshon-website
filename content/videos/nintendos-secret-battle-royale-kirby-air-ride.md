Kirby Air Ride is a racing game like no other, with gameplay basically boiling down to the mastery of one skill: drifting.

Directed by Kirby's creator and Smash Bros director, Masahiro Sakurai and released for the Nintendo GameCube in 2003, the game is a departure from the traditional side-scrolling of the franchise and has become a cult classic.
