## Inside Fleta's Tracer Stealth Plays

One standout moment came on Dorado, where Fleta crouched completely out of sight on the roof. While the enemy team poked for picks, he sat motionless, reading their patterns. Pro players win fights by understanding *when* to engage, not just how fast they can click.

## Why Tracer Stealth Beats Constant Aggression

Many Tracer mains fall into the trap of nonstop aggression. Fleta shows that the hero's real power lies in threat denial and positioning advantage. By staying hidden until the enemy commits resources elsewhere, Tracer forces teams to play reactively. They can't rotate, they can't push, and they can't execute their plan—all because one player is lurking somewhere they don't expect.

## Micro Plays That Turn Team Fights

Micro plays—small, overlooked moments—often decide who claims the objective. Fleta's stealth approach is the opposite of highlight-reel chaos. Instead of trading kills, he creates space by removing a key threat before anyone knows he's there. Other pro Tracer players might farm multi-kills, but a single positioning-based pick can shift the momentum of an entire round.

## Learning From Pro Overwatch Positioning

Watch how Fleta uses cover, roof angles, and natural sightlines to stay undetected. He doesn't panic when enemies swarm nearby—he uses that chaos as camouflage. Studying these micro plays helps players at any rank sharpen game sense and recognize when to play for positioning versus fighting for kills. Rewatch the moments where he holds his blink and recall for escape, not engage—it's a subtle but crucial habit.
