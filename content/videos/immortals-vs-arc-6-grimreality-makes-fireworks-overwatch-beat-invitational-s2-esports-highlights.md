# Immortals vs Arc 6: Grim Reality Tracer Carry Highlights

Grim Reality's Tracer carry performance was the difference-maker in Immortals' dominant 2-0 series victory over Arc 6 at the Overwatch Beat Invitational S2. From clutch pulse bomb timing to relentless backline pressure, his mechanical skill and game sense turned close fights into decisive wins. Every map showcased why elite Tracer play remains one of the most impactful performances in competitive Overwatch esports.

## Tracer Carry Mechanics and Positioning

What made Grim Reality's Tracer carry so devastating was his positioning sense. He consistently found openings against Arc 6's backline, pressuring supports and forcing defensive cooldowns before committing fully to engagements. His ability to bait out enemy abilities—especially transcendences and defensive matrices—created space for Immortals' frontline to dominate.

The timing on his pulse bombs was surgical. Whether catching Zenyatta mid-rotation or stunning enemies during critical fights, Grim's bombs arrived at moments that shifted momentum. Against a team like Arc 6, who relied on support ults for sustainability, these early picks were fight-winning.

## Coordinate Flanking and Map Control

Immortals' overall coordination amplified Grim's impact. While his Tracer hunted the backline, Agilities' Genji and Fate's Winston created a suffocating offensive. On Ilios and King's Row, this synergy proved especially deadly—Grim's flanks opened space for main tank aggression, while Fate's aggressive dives forced Arc 6 into reactive gameplay.

Arc 6 attempted multiple hero rotations and positioning adjustments, but Immortals' execution remained consistent. When Arc 6 members got caught out of position, there was no second chance. Grim was already dealing critical damage, keeping opponents in constant survival mode rather than allowing proactive aggression.

## Support Play and Transcendence Timing

Beyond Grim's Tracer carry, Immortals' support line proved essential. Kusta's Zenyatta transcendence timing consistently negated Arc 6's burst potential during critical dive windows. These support plays often went unnoticed but directly enabled Grim's aggressive positioning and allowed him to take fights Tracer normally couldn't survive.

Arc 6's K showed moments of brilliance on Zenyatta, hitting crucial transcendences post-EMP that kept fights alive momentarily. However, consistency favored Immortals, whose supports seemed one step ahead in fight prediction and cooldown management throughout the series.

## Overtime Excellence and Clutch Execution

The series came down to execution in overtime scenarios, where Grim's Tracer carry truly shined. With maps hanging in the balance, his pulse bomb accuracy and target prioritization forced Arc 6's hand repeatedly. Final rounds on Ilios demonstrated why mechanical perfection matters—one miscalculation from Arc 6 meant instant punishment.

Immortals' ability to snowball early advantages and close out maps before overtime became necessary showed championship-level discipline. Grim's contribution extended beyond highlight reel kills; his presence forced Arc 6 to respect him constantly, opening opportunities for his teammates.
