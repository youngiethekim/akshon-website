## What Made the Grim Reality Tracer Carry So Deadly

The difference wasn't just raw aim — it was positioning. Grim consistently found seams in Arc 6's backline, pressuring supports and baiting defensive cooldowns before fully committing. By forcing out transcendences and defense matrices early, he created space for Immortals' frontline to snowball. His pulse bombs arrived at fight-deciding moments, catching Zenyatta mid-rotation and removing Arc 6's sustain before teamfights even started.

## Coordinated Flanks and Map Control

Grim didn't carry alone. Agilities' Genji and Fate's Winston built a suffocating offensive rhythm, and on Ilios and King's Row that synergy turned lethal. Arc 6 tried hero swaps and repositioning, but every mistake was punished instantly — Immortals' execution never wavered, keeping opponents in survival mode rather than letting them dictate fights.
