Metroid Prime Hunters is an action-adventure game developed by Nintendo Software Technology and was the perfect game to showcase the capabilities of the Nintendo DS.

Despite some criticism of the game's control scheme and lackluster single-player mode compared to previous entries in the franchise, Metroid Prime Hunters proved to be incredibly successful with over 410,000 copies sold in North America in its first month of release, and has been widely praised for its multiplayer gameplay.

In today's video, we'll be taking a look at Metroid Prime Hunters and how this underrated Nintendo FPS with an incredible multiplayer mode has withstood the test of time.
