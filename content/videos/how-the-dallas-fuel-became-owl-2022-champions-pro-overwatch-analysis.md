# Dallas Fuel 2022 OWL Champions: How They Won

The 2022 Overwatch League grand finals delivered one of the most thrilling championship matches in competitive Overwatch history. Dallas Fuel, the longtime underdogs, clashed with the dominant San Francisco Shock in a best-of-seven series that came down to pure strategy, mechanical skill, and mental resilience. Here's how the Fuel dismantled the two-time defending champions.

## The Setup: Two Powerhouses Collide

Heading into the finals, San Francisco looked nearly unstoppable. They cruised through the winners bracket without dropping more than one map in any match, featuring star players like Proper, Striker, and Violet. Dallas, meanwhile, had just finished their own dominant run through the lower bracket with legends like Fearless, Sparkle, and Fielder anchoring the team. Edison's Sojourn play had been refined to perfection, making the Fuel look like the team everyone had been waiting for all season.

## Map 1: Li Jang Tower and Opening Picks

Both teams opened with mirror compositions featuring Reaper, Sojourn, Winston, Kiriko, and Lucio. The key difference? Mechanical execution and positioning. Dallas demonstrated superior awareness in crucial moments, especially in securing opening picks against Shock's backline. Fearless's aggressive tank play forced Shock into reactive positions, and when Fielder landed crucial boops to save the Fuel trio mid-fight, momentum began shifting. The Fuel's ability to make winning decisions in desperate situations—their recipe for success—was already on display.

## The Shock Strikes Back: Proper's Dominance

As the series progressed, San Francisco revealed their game plan: make Proper an unkillable one-shot machine. On Dorado, Proper's Sojourn positioning and support coordination became oppressive. He slid into the Fuel backline repeatedly, with Finn's immortality field and Violet's sound barrier keeping him alive through coordinated focus-fire. The Fuel were forced to dedicate massive resources just to eliminate him, leaving their own win conditions vulnerable. This defensive strategy nearly secured map three for the Shock.

## The Turning Point: Route 66 and the Timeout

Down 3-2 in maps, Dallas called a crucial timeout after losing Oasis. In those critical moments, the Fuel completely reformatted their approach. Instead of chasing Proper, they decided to protect Edison at all costs—mirroring Shock's own strategy. On Route 66, this new game plan immediately paid dividends. When Edison took aggressive high-ground positions, Fearless provided bubble protection rather than pressing forward. Most critically, when Edison and Fearless positioned together with bubble coverage, Edison could safely land rail gun shots without fear of being picked. On the final fight of Route 66, Edison eliminated Proper while Fearless protected him with the bubble—turning the Shock's own playbook against them.

## Game Seven: Coliseum and Championship Glory

With the series tied 3-3, both teams headed into Coliseum knowing this final map would determine the champion. Dallas's refined strategy and mental fortitude in the final game secured the title. The Fuel's ability to adapt mid-series, learn from defeat, and execute under pressure proved to be the difference-maker.
