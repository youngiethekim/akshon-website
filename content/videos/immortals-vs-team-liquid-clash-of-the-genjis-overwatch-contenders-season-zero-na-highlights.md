# Immortals vs Team Liquid: Genji Clash for the Ages

Immorals and Team Liquid faced off in one of Overwatch Contenders Season Zero's most competitive matchups, and the Genji play throughout this series was absolutely electric. Both teams were hunting for first place, the better seed into Season One, and the cash that came with it. This clash between top-tier flex DPS players like Shatter2K and Agilities showcased why Genji dueling remains one of the most exciting mechanics in competitive Overwatch.

## Nepal: Immortals Build Early Advantage

Immorals came out swinging with aggressive dive plays that caught Team Liquid off-guard from the opening fight. The Genji duel exchanges early on demonstrated why both Shatter2K and Agilities are considered top-tier. Team Liquid's Zenyatta support K was crucial in keeping his team alive during these chaotic dives, but Immortals' consistency in securing picks on the point forced TL into a defensive posture. The ultimate economy advantage that Immortals built early set the tone for the entire round, giving them space to control the map.

## Junkenstein's Revenge: Payload Control Shifts

When the series moved to escort, the dynamic changed dramatically. Team Liquid's structured approach to payload defense showed they'd studied Immortals' tendencies. The Tracer and Sombra swaps brought an entirely different layer to the competition. K's Transcendence timing became critical—one poorly-timed ultimate could hand Team Liquid the round. Meanwhile, Grim Reality's pulse bomb accuracy on key targets like Zenyatta threatened to unravel TL's defensive setup. What made this series compelling was how both teams adapted mid-fight.

## Hanamura: Defensive Discipline and EMP Disruption

The symmetrical map brought out the best in both rosters. Immortals' full hold on defense showed disciplined positioning and ultimate management. When Team Liquid switched to a Sombra-focused attack, it became clear they were trying to counter Immortals' dive threat with EMP hacks and disruption. However, Immortals' flexibility—swapping to Widowmaker briefly and adjusting their defensive stance—proved just enough to secure crucial rounds.

## Hollywood: Genji Mechanics and Blade Timing

By the final map, both teams were feeling the pressure. This is where individual Genji mechanical skill truly separated play. Shatter2K and Agilities continued trading blade kills, but positioning and team coordination determined who came out on top in each team fight. The nano-boosted Genji plays, the well-timed Transcendences, and the perfectly-placed Dragon Blade angles all contributed to an intense, back-and-forth battle that came down to inches.
