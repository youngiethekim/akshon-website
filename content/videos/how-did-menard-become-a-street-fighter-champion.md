Who is MenaRD and how did he become a Street Fighter 6 champion?

Saul Leonardo "MenaRD" Mena II is a veteran Street Fighter player from the Dominican Republic. No stranger to competition, in the first half of 2024, he has already won Red Bull Kumite and EVO Japan to cement himself as one of the world's best Street Fighter 6 players.

In this video, we explore MenaRD's playstyle, his signature Blanka, and what it takes to be one of the best in the world.
