The MenaRD Street Fighter champion story reached its peak on March 17th, 2024, when he won Red Bull Kumite—one of the fighting game community's most prestigious invitationals. The two-time Capcom Cup winner is the first Latin American player to claim the title, and his run showed more than raw skill. It revealed an adaptive mentality and an unorthodox Blanca play style that is reshaping how the character is played in Street Fighter 6.

## How the MenaRD Street Fighter Champion Run Began

MenaRD's path was built on preparation, not luck. He studied opponents through exhibition matches and leaned on region-specific specialists for tips. His aggressive, rushdown-heavy Blanca forces opponents into hyperreactive decisions, favoring his confidence in reading the moment. That bulldog approach separates him from other top players.

In his opening set against Broski, a UK streamer on the low-tier Aki, MenaRD faced the element of surprise. Aki lacks a traditional wake-up reversal, making her rare at a high level. After warm-up matches and advice from a Puerto Rican Aki main, MenaRD adapted mid-set and won 9-1.

## Blanca Gameplay That Dominated the Bracket

Against GachiKun in Round 2, MenaRD switched to Blanca and delivered a one-sided performance. His mastery of Blanca's grab ranges and frame-perfect punishes overwhelmed GachiKun's Luke, proving his character knowledge runs deeper than most.

The semifinals brought Punk, the tournament favorite. Yet Punk has admitted the Blanca-versus-Cammy matchup is nearly unwinnable for him. MenaRD crushed him 5-0, showcasing preparation few competitors can match.

## The Mental Edge That Sealed It

What truly sets MenaRD apart is his adaptive mentality. He plays without headphones, relying on visual cues to maximize elite reaction times. He also thrives on crowd energy and pressure, using skepticism as fuel instead of letting doubt creep in. This resilience, plus the ability to flip between offense and defense instantly, makes him nearly impossible to counter-pick.

In Grand Finals against North American veteran Knuckle-Do, watching MenaRD dismantle Punk cracked his confidence. He abandoned his Cammy, switched to Guile, and walked into a matchup MenaRD knows intimately through training with best friend and elite Guile player Cabba. The 5-0 win was decided before it began.

## The Blanca Meta Shift After His Win

After Red Bull Kumite, top Japanese players like Nemo quickly picked up Blanca, echoing the strategy that countered Punk back in Street Fighter 5. But MenaRD's play style is fundamentally different from how other Blanca players approach the character, so copying the pick alone won't guarantee results. His light-switch toggling confuses opponents who expect consistency.
