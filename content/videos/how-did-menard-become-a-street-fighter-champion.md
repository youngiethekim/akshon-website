MenaRD became a Street Fighter champion on March 17th, 2024, when he won Red Bull Kumite—one of the fighting game community's most prestigious tournaments. The two-time Capcom Cup champion is the first Latin American player to claim this invitational title, and his victory showcases more than raw technical skill. It reveals a unique mentality and play style that's reshaping how Blanca is played in Street Fighter 6.

## How MenaRD Became a Street Fighter Champion

MenaRD's path to becoming a Street Fighter champion wasn't handed to him. He prepared extensively before Red Bull Kumite, studying opponents through exhibition matches and absorbing tips from region-specific specialists. His aggressive, rushdown-heavy Blanca forces opponents into hyperreactive decision-making, favoring his confidence in making the right read. This bulldog-like approach distinguishes him from other top players.

In his first match against Broski—a UK streamer playing the low-tier character Aki—MenaRD faced the element of surprise. Aki lacks a traditional wake-up reversal, making her unusual at high-level play. However, after exhibition matches with Broski and advice from a Puerto Rican Aki player, MenaRD adapted mid-set and won 9-1.

## Blanca Gameplay That Dominated Round Two

Against GachiKun in Round 2, MenaRD switched to Blanca and delivered a one-sided performance. His understanding of Blanca's grab ranges and frame-perfect punishes overwhelmed GachiKun's Luke. This victory proved that MenaRD's character knowledge runs deeper than most players.

The semifinals brought punk, widely considered the tournament favorite. Yet punk has repeatedly admitted that Blanca's matchup against his Cammy is nearly unwinnable. MenaRD crushed punk 5-0, showcasing preparation that few competitors can match.

## Mental Edge and Psychological Resilience

What truly separates MenaRD is his adaptive mentality. He plays without headphones, relying purely on visual cues to maximize elite reaction times. More importantly, he thrives on crowd energy and pressure, using skepticism as fuel rather than letting doubt creep in. This psychological resilience, combined with his ability to switch between offensive and defensive modes instantly, makes him nearly impossible to counter-pick.

In Grand Finals, MenaRD faced knuckled-do, a North American veteran. However, watching MenaRD dismantle punk affected knuckled-do's confidence. When he returned to character select, he doubted his Cammy matchup and switched to Guile—a character where MenaRD has extensive experience through training with his best friend and fellow top-two Guile player, cabba. MenaRD's 5-0 victory was essentially decided before the set began.

## The Blanca Meta Shift After His Victory

After Red Bull Kumite, top Japanese players like Nemo quickly picked up Blanca, mimicking the strategy that countered punk in Street Fighter 5. However, MenaRD's unorthodox play style—fundamentally different from how other Blanca players approach the character—means simply copying the character won't guarantee success. His ability to toggle between playstyles like a light switch confuses opponents who expect consistency.
