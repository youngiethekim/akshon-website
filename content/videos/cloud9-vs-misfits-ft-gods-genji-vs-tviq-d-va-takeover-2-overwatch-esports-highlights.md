The second day of TaKeOver 2 lives up to the hype from day one with high level competition featuring matches between teams such as Cloud9, Misfits, Hollywood Hammers, and Moviestar Riders.

Starting off the day, Cloud9 went up against Misfits with fans on the edge of their seats wondering which team had the upper hand. With Cloud9 and Misfits have both played in the second of APEX and being knocked out of the group stage, the two have yet to face one another. In this match, Gods stepped up his Genji play and silenced the critics after a poor showing at the Overwatch Rumble last May. The same could not be said for Misfits as they struggled to tame the beast that was Cloud9 and was overwhelmed.

TaKeOver 2 is the most competitive Western tournament since the Overwatch Open last year with the strongest teams competing for a prize pool of $50,000 and of course bragging rights. TaKeOver 2 is the successor to the first Overwatch LAN in the west and is a refreshing break from the lack of tournaments in the EU region. The tournament features: Rogue, Ninjas In Pyjamas (NiP), eUnited, Cyclowns, Movistar Riders, Cloud 9, Misfits, and Hollywood Hammers.

The eight teams have been split into two groups of four. Each of these groups will compete in a round-robin stage played as a four-map set. The top three teams from each group will advance into a double elimination bracket.
