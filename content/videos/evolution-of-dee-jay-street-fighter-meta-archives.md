Dee Jay, a kickboxing karateka hailing from Jamaica, is a long-standing character of the Street Fighter franchise who made his debut in Super Street Fighter II (1993).

Over the course of several titles, Dee Jay has been considered a low-tier character, but Dee Jay's return in Street Fighter 6 looks to turn things around.

In today's video, we take a look at the evolution of Dee Jay in the Street Fighter franchise and the history of his relevance in competitive play.
