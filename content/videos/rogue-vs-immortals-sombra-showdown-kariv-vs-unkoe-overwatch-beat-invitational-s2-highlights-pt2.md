Highlights of the grand finals of the Overwatch Beat Invitational Season 2 between Rogue and Immortals. Rogue headed into this series from the winner's bracket after knocking out Immortals in the semifinals. However, after taking down Arc 6 in a dominant 3-0 sweep, IMT were back with a vengeance and their sights set on victory.

IMT came into the fourth map of the series down 3-0, but as the Loser's, a map win on Lijiang Tower helped them secure their first victory. Notable players in this series are KariV and uNKOE whose Sombra plays made or break certain teamfights in this series. IMT demonstrated why they are one of the top three teams in North American in this series with their execeptional ultimate economy management and excellent teamwork.
