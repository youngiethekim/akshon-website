Why to Super Smash Bros Melee players prefer gaming on old televisions instead of modern day monitors?

Compared to other esports events, SSBM tournaments are an outlier in the longevity of the title and in terms of setups with the unique prevalence of cathode ray tube (CRT) televisions.

Is it nostalgia? Is it the responsive timing? In today's video, we look into why SSBM players still play on huge CRT TVs.
