Blanka is a character in Capcom's Street Fighter series with a self-taught, animalistic combat style whose kit features quick movement, long-range normals and an iconic Electric Thunder attack

He was first introduced in 1991 as one of eight playable characters in Street Fighter II and has since had multiple appearances across subsequent sequels and spinoffs.

In today's video, we will explore the history of Blanka, his lore, and how this character has fared in competitive play over the years in the Street Fighter franchise.
