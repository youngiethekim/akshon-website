# Blanka Street Fighter History: From Feral Beast to S-Tier

Blanka is one of Street Fighter's most iconic and unique characters, and his journey from obscure charge character to legitimate competitive threat is nothing short of remarkable. From his mysterious origins in Brazil to his transformation into a tournament-viable fighter in Street Fighter 6, this Brazilian jungle warrior has captivated players for decades. Whether you're new to the series or a hardcore fan, understanding Blanka's evolution reveals how Capcom balanced lore, gameplay, and character design across multiple generations.

## The Origin Story: How Jimmy Carlos Became Blanka

Blanka wasn't always green or feral. Originally named Jimmy Carlos, he was a normal Brazilian man until a catastrophic plane crash changed everything. According to Street Fighter lore, his aircraft was shot down by Shadaloo while flying over the Amazon rainforest. Stranded in the jungle, Jimmy survived and encountered electric eels, which granted him his signature electric-based powers. The game's developers wanted to move beyond purely humanoid characters in Street Fighter 2, so they transformed Jimmy into the beast we know today. His green skin and wild appearance make him instantly recognizable—so much so that a 2008 Spanish survey found him as recognizable as Pelé and Gisele Bündchen.

## Blanka in Street Fighter 2: Mastering the Charge Character

Blanka's Street Fighter 2 playstyle revolves around three core tools: electricity, rolling attacks, and his devastating bite. As a charge character, he excels at controlling space and forcing opponents into defensive positions. His electricity move is particularly clever—if opponents touch him during the animation, they're knocked down, making it invaluable against cross-up heavy characters. His standing kicks and crouching punches offer solid anti-air coverage. Despite his unique toolkit and gimmicks, Blanka remained low-to-mid tier due to challenging matchups.

## From Street Fighter Alpha to Street Fighter 4

Blanka appeared in Street Fighter Alpha 3, where new lore revealed his friendship with Dan Hibiki after saving his life in the Amazon. He made cameo appearances across numerous crossover titles including Capcom vs. SNK, Street Fighter X Tekken, and even a brief appearance in Wreck-It Ralph. However, Street Fighter 4 proved difficult for Blanka players. While his mixup potential seemed strong, unsafe options and poor defensive capabilities made him unreliable at competitive levels.

## The Street Fighter 5 Disaster

Blanka hit rock bottom in Street Fighter 5. Professional players ranked him F-tier—literally the worst character in the game. He lacked pressure strings, had impossibly bad matchups, and received minimal tournament representation. The character desperately needed help.

## Street Fighter 6: The Complete Blanka Overhaul

Street Fighter 6 changed everything for this jungle warrior. Capcom gave Blanka a complete overhaul with tons of mixup tools, safe rolling attacks for combos, improved anti-air coverage, and a projectile-based mechanic using his signature Blanka Chan doll. His EX moves became plus-on-block threats. For the first time in decades, Blanka became genuinely viable at the highest competitive level. At EVO 2023, professional player MenaRD used Blanka to achieve a bracket reset in Grand Finals, proving the character's newfound strength and competitive viability.
