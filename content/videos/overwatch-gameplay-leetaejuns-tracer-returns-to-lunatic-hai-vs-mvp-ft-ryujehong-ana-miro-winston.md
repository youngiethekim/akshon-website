This Overwatch gameplay highlights Lunatic-Hai's Tracer Leetaejun's return VS MVP Space at the Overwatch OGN APEX S4. Featuring some of the best players in the world like Ryujehong on Ana, Miro on Winston, MVP's DPS star Onefact did an admirable job in this crucial group stage match with Doomfist. Also included in this video is our interview with MVP's sub healer, Undine.

After a controversial leave, Lunatic-Hai's original member Leetaejun made his return on Friday, pairing up with EscA to form the DPS duo we have known for so long. Despite being out of the pro scene for months, Leetaejun has been keeping himself sharp, peaking at rank 1 on Overwatch's Asia servers.

OGN APEX is a major Overwatch tournament that has featured some of the best players in the world like NRG Seagull, EnVyUs Taimou (McCree, Widowmaker), Mickie (D.Va), HarryHook (Soldier:76), Rogue aKm, SoOn, Cloud9 Surefour, Mendokusaii, Lunatic-Hai Miro (Winston), Ryujehong (Ana), WhoRU (Genji), zunba, LW BLUE Nanohana or Flow3r, Saebyeolbe, Kongdoo Rascal, Birdring, and RunAway Runner, Kaiser, Haksal, among others.
