Speedrunning at its most fundamental level is when a player attempts to complete a video game or break a certain record as quickly as possible.

In today's video, we will showcase a series of techniques from the Backwards Longjump in Super Mario 64 to the Wrong Warp in Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time that, upon discovery, have become iconic in speedrunning history.
