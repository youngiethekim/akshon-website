Today's video will look at a fighting game franchise that is receiving a much-needed second chance.

Developed by Arc System Works and published by Cygames, Granblue Fantasy Versus: Rising is a sequel to Granblue Fantasy Versus.

After the first game failed to garner much attention due to poor net code, Rising has provided the serious a much-needed second chance within the fighting game community.

From being a fun and simple pick-up game to having a free-to-play version that allows anyone to try the game out, Rising has done everything right so far and is only continuing to grow.
