# MkLeo: The Greatest Smash Ultimate Player of All Time

Leonardo Lopez, better known as MkLeo, stands as the greatest Smash Ultimate player to ever grace the competitive scene. From humble beginnings in Mexico City to becoming a generational talent, MkLeo's story is one of determination, skill, and unwavering dominance.

## Rising from Mexico City

Born in 2001, MkLeo started playing Smash Bros at just five or six years old, but competitive play came later. Growing up in Naucalpan, one of Mexico's most dangerous cities, MkLeo found refuge in gaming. His family struggled financially—they often had to pawn belongings just to afford food—but this adversity only strengthened his resolve. When Brawl released, his entire family began attending tournaments, and MkLeo followed suit. At just seven years old, he was competing; by eight, he'd already defeated Mexico's number one ranked player.

## The Journey to Dominance

When Smash 4 launched on Wii U, MkLeo's brother made him an offer: perform well, and he'd fund his travel to bigger tournaments. This life-changing opportunity came with a condition—and a name. At his first major Smash 4 event, MkLeo initially played Marth, but after struggling early, he switched to Meta Knight. The character choice proved pivotal. Though Meta Knight was later nerfed, MkLeo had already proven his potential.

For years, the Mexican smash community remained overlooked. However, everything changed in 2015 when Mr R, a top European Smash 4 player and second-place finisher at Evo, traveled to Mexico. The world expected him to dominate. Instead, the then-14-year-old MkLeo demolished him 3-1 in winners and 3-0 in Grand Finals. This victory caught the attention of the competitive world and signaled that Mexico—and MkLeo—were forces to be reckoned with.

## The Ultimate Era Begins

When Super Smash Bros. Ultimate released in December 2018, MkLeo was ready. Sponsored by T1, one of esports' most prestigious organizations, he could finally travel to every major tournament. His early Ultimate career saw fierce competition with Tweek, but after winning Genesis 6, MkLeo entered a remarkable streak. He won MomoCon, Smash and Splash, and CEO back-to-back-to-back.

Then came Evo 2019—the tournament that cemented his legacy. After a grueling run through the bracket, MkLeo faced Tweek in Grand Finals. In one of the greatest sets in Smash history, MkLeo won the first set in a stunning Game 4 comeback, reset the bracket, and claimed victory 3-0 in the second set. This performance proved he wasn't just a contender—he was the undisputed best in the world.

## Unmatched Consistency

Following Evo 2019, MkLeo's dominance became almost unreal. At one point, he placed first at 30 of his last 37 tournaments, consistently reaching Grand Finals across majors worldwide. The online era of 2020-2021 temporarily slowed his momentum, but he proved his caliber remained supreme upon returning to in-person competition, winning tournaments like Low Tide City and Genesis 8 with diverse character picks including Joker, Byleth, Corrin, and Aegis.

While newer players like Spargo and Gluttony have emerged as formidable challengers, MkLeo remains the greatest Smash Ultimate player of all time—a title earned through years of consistent excellence, character mastery, and an unshakeable competitive spirit.
