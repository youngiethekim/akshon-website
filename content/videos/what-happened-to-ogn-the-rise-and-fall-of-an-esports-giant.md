As a pioneer in esports broadcasting, OGN for decades was known for having raised the bar when it comes to esports, content, tournaments and production quality.

Whether it be broadcasting League of Legends on TV or hosting Overwatch APEX, OGN was a company that focused on showcasing talent and succeeded in elevated esports storytelling across the industry.

Despite their sterling track record, a bevy of international fans, and being one of the longest-standing names in esports, it has been reported that OGN will be shutting down for good.
