Why is Super Smash Bros considered to be the best platform fighter of all time?

Super Smash Bros. is widely regarded as the best platform fighter, boasting a genre-defining reputation. Featuring a massive crossover roster of beloved Nintendo characters, deep mechanics that offer a high skill ceiling, accessibility for casual play as a party game, Melee has gone down in history for its immense cultural impact, inspiring many other games while maintaining a dedicated fanbase with a passionate competitive scene.
