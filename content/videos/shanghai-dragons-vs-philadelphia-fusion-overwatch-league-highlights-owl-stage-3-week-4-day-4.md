# Shanghai Dragons vs Philadelphia Fusion OWL Highlights

The Shanghai Dragons and Philadelphia Fusion delivered an intense Overwatch League matchup on Blizzard World that showcased world-class mechanical skill, tactical flexibility, and nerve-wracking overtime fights. ## Dive Composition and Early Positioning

Philadelphia started on defense with an unexpected pure dive composition, but quickly pivoted to a widow-based setup when Shanghai pressed in. This early flexibility set the tone for a match that would demand constant adaptation. The opening engagements saw carpe and the Fusion's DPS line hunting for picks, while Shanghai's tank line—led by fearless—looked to secure space on the point.

## The Resurrection Economy Decides Fights

One of the most critical differences between these two teams came down to resurrection management. Philadelphia's supports, led by Neptuno, landed clean resurrections that kept their team alive through extended fights. Meanwhile, Shanghai's altering faced challenges keeping up with the resurrection output, forcing the Dragons to lose crucial players in mid-fight scenarios. This support differential became the deciding factor in several engagements.

Zenyatta's transcendence became a major factor, with altering timing crucial defensive ults at pivotal moments. ## Overtime Battles and Clutch Moments

The final push descended into chaotic overtime, with both teams burning through every ultimate ability. The match came down to positioning, healing output, and who could land the final picks when both teams were running on fumes.

Shanghai eventually flipped the point through overwhelming aggression and superior positioning in the final seconds, demonstrating the kind of high-level Overwatch that separates elite teams from the rest.
