## Kruise's Dragon Blade: Timing Over Mechanics

What separates professional Overwatch from ranked is understanding *when* to use ultimate, not just *how*. **Kruise's Dragon Blade** becomes the turning point in multiple critical teamfights. Rather than forcing blade early, Kruise waits for his team to create the perfect setup—then delivers cleanup kills that end engagements decisively. His dash resets swing momentum repeatedly, showcasing why he's a threat every time his ultimate comes online.

On maps like Oasis and Route 66, Kruise demonstrates patience and positioning. He doesn't chase kills blindly; he isolates targets his teammates have already weakened. That restraint is what turns a good Genji into a game-winning one.

By the final map engagements, the difference is stark. This is where **Overwatch esports** separates the elite from the rest. eUnited's players know exactly when to spend resources and when to hold.
